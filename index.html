The professional network for healthcare

Where medical professionals and healthcare facilities connect.

Locumfy brings the whole working life of a clinician into one place — a verified professional profile, a network of peers, relevant job opportunities, direct messaging, and the timesheets that turn an assignment into recorded, approved work.

  • Credentials cross-checked against official healthcare records
  • A mobile app for clinicians and a full web portal for facilities
  • One continuous path from profile to approved hours
Why Locumfy exists

Healthcare careers run on scattered data and manual work.

Clinicians are spread across profession-specific job boards, resumes are reformatted by hand for every submission, and the work that follows a placement lives in a different system entirely. Locumfy closes those gaps.

One professional home

A nurse, therapist, physician, or allied health professional builds a single profile that follows them across roles and years — not a throwaway application for one job.

Resume work, automated

The parsing engine reads a resume, structures it into eighteen searchable sections, and produces a clean, facility-ready document — with personal details removed on request.

The whole lifecycle

Finding the role, verifying credentials, working the assignment, and logging approved hours all happen in the same platform, with every stage captured as structured data.

Two audiences, one network

The same platform, tailored to each side of the market.

Medical professionals and facilities see tools designed for what they each do — but they share one network, one messaging system, and one set of verified profiles.

The engine underneath

A resume becomes a verified, searchable profile.

Locumfy reads resumes in PDF and Word form, structures what it finds into eighteen distinct sections, then matches the result against the national provider registry and public credential records. What comes out is a profile that a facility can act on with confidence — and that the platform can match against open roles.

The same structured data powers the other direction too: a standardized, facility-ready resume generated on demand, with names, contact details and employers removed when a submission calls for it.
